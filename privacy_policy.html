Oddly Clean Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 (applies from version 1.1.0)

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Oddly Clean ("the app"), published by Hongsup Shim ("we", "us"), handles your information.

Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information, and we run no servers. The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, contacts or photos. The app does show ads, provided by Google AdMob, which collects some information as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (for example coins, completed levels, collection, brush skins, missions and settings) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded ads (for example to double a reward) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device (model, operating system, language) and how you interact with ads,
  • diagnostic information such as crash and performance data.

This data is handled by Google as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive information that identifies you.

Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences (PG or lower).

Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ads are shown, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent message. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy settings in the app's Shop.
  • Personalized ads: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase removes all ads from the app.

In-app purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase was made, so ads can be removed (also after reinstalling the app).

Permissions

  • Internet and advertising ID: to load ads and check purchases with Google Play.
  • Billing: to offer the Remove Ads purchase through Google Play.
  • Vibrate: for haptic feedback while cleaning.

Third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob (ads), Google User Messaging Platform (consent) and Google Play Billing (purchases), and is distributed through Google Play. Each is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the "Last updated" date above.
